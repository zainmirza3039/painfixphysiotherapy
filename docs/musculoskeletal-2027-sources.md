# Sources for “Musculoskeletal Problems to Watch in 2027”

Editorial source notes for the PainFix static blog article.

1. World Health Organization, “Musculoskeletal health” — global burden, rehabilitation need, low back pain, osteoarthritis, fractures, sarcopenia, ageing and future low back pain burden.
   https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/musculoskeletal-conditions

2. World Health Organization, “Low back pain” — 619 million people, leading cause of disability, approximately 90% non-specific cases, risk factors and rehabilitation/self-care guidance.
   https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/low-back-pain

3. World Health Organization, “Nearly 1.8 billion adults at risk of disease from not doing enough physical activity” — 31% of adults / 1.8 billion in 2022 and a projected 35% inactivity level by 2030 if the trend continues.
   https://www.who.int/news/item/26-06-2024-nearly-1.8-billion-adults-at-risk-of-disease-from-not-doing-enough-physical-activity

4. Steinmetz et al., “Global, regional, and national burden of osteoarthritis, 1990–2020 and projections to 2050,” The Lancet Rheumatology / PubMed — 595 million people in 2020 and projected increases for knee and hip osteoarthritis by 2050.
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37675071/

5. International Labour Organization, “ILO supports inclusion of musculoskeletal disorders into the list of occupational diseases in China” — work-related MSDs, ergonomic and psychosocial risks, and work-loss context.
   https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/ilo-supports-inclusion-musculoskeletal-disorders-list-occupational-diseases

Editorial note: The article presents population-level pressures likely to remain important in 2027. It does not claim that a specific new disease will appear or predict an individual patient’s diagnosis.
