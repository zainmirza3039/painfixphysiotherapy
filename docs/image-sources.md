## Implementation notes
- Photographs are served from Pexels CDN with width/quality parameters; they are not copied into the repository. This avoids rehosting third-party binary files in this pass, but means image delivery depends on Pexels CDN availability.
- Local PainFix clinic photographs, staff portraits, and local anatomy/symptom diagrams remain unchanged.
- All stock-photo ALT text explicitly distinguishes representative stock from genuine PainFix clinic imagery.
- Before making these the final permanent image library, PainFix can replace stock with its own consented treatment photographs.
